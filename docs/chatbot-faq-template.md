# Chatbot FAQ Templates for Filipino SMBs

Use these templates to train your chatbot. These are common questions Filipino customers ask.

## 🍽️ Restaurants
- **Saan po kayo banda?** (Location)
- **May parking po ba?** (Parking availability)
- **Ano po ang best seller niyo?** (Menu recommendations)
- **Open po ba kayo kapag holiday?** (Holiday hours)
- **Tumatanggap po ba kayo ng GCash?** (Payment methods)

## 🩺 Clinics
- **Magkano po ang consultation fee?** (Pricing)
- **Kailangan po ba ng appointment?** (Booking process)
- **Ano pong oras ang clinic ni Doc?** (Doctor's schedule)
- **Tumatanggap po ba kayo ng HMO/Maxicare?** (Insurance)

## 💇 Salons & Spas
- **Magkano po ang gupit/rebond?** (Service rates)
- **Saan po pwede mag-book?** (Appointments)
- **Pwedeng walk-in?** (Walk-in policy)
- **May promo po ba kayo?** (Current offers)

## 🚜 Farms & Local Stores
- **Magkano po per kilo?** (Pricing)
- **Nagde-deliver po ba kayo sa [Area]?** (Delivery coverage)
- **Minimum order po?** (Order requirements)
- **Sariwa po ba ang mga ito?** (Product quality)

## 💡 Pro-Tip for Pinoy Businesses
Filipino customers appreciate a mix of English and Tagalog (Taglish). Make sure your bot understands both and responds in a friendly, "maka-masa" tone!
