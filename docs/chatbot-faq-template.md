# Chatbot FAQ Templates for Filipino SMBs

Use these starter questions to train the bot. Replace answers with verified client information before launch.

## Restaurants

- Saan po kayo banda? (Location)
- May parking po ba? (Parking availability)
- Ano po ang best seller niyo? (Menu recommendations)
- Open po ba kayo kapag holiday? (Holiday hours)
- Tumatanggap po ba kayo ng GCash or card? (Payment methods)
- Pwede po ba magpa-reserve? (Reservations)
- Nagde-deliver po ba kayo? (Delivery coverage)

## Clinics

- Magkano po ang consultation fee? (Pricing)
- Kailangan po ba ng appointment? (Booking process)
- Ano pong oras ang clinic ni Doc? (Doctor schedule)
- Tumatanggap po ba kayo ng HMO? (Insurance)
- Ano po ang requirements before appointment? (Patient preparation)

## Salons and Spas

- Magkano po ang gupit or rebond? (Service rates)
- Saan po pwede mag-book? (Appointments)
- Pwedeng walk-in? (Walk-in policy)
- May promo po ba kayo? (Current offers)
- Gaano katagal ang service? (Service duration)

## Farms and Local Stores

- Magkano po per kilo? (Pricing)
- Nagde-deliver po ba kayo sa [Area]? (Delivery coverage)
- Minimum order po? (Order requirements)
- Sariwa po ba ang products? (Product quality)
- Paano po mag-order? (Ordering process)

## Training Tips

- Add both English and Tagalog examples.
- Include common misspellings and casual Taglish phrasing.
- Keep answers short and actionable.
- Add a fallback that offers phone, email, or human support.
- Do not promise payment, booking, or delivery actions unless those workflows are actually connected.
